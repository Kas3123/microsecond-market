Pyramid Quote Lab

A market maker posts stacked buy and sell orders around the price and earns the gap. Move the sliders to see how quote width, ladder depth and risk aversion change fills, inventory and profit. Volatility is calibrated to Liquid's live NASDAQ‑100 reading (ATR 83.34 pts/hour at 30,911). Prices are simulated.

Your chosen settings (4× width, 7 levels, taper 0.6, γ 0.54) run on 50 price paths this page never uses (seeds 1000–1049). Every run is listed below. Click a column header to sort.

Model: stationary Avellaneda‑Stoikov. Reservation price r = mid − q·γ·σ²/κ; half‑spread δ = γσ²/(2κ) + (1/γ)·ln(1+γ/κ), with κ = 1.5 and σ the rolling 5‑minute volatility. Fills come from price moving through a quote (informed flow) or from random arrivals at rate A·e^(−κ·distance). Latency and queue position are not modelled.
